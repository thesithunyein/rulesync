# Code of conduct

RuleSync is a small project. The bar is the same as the product: say what you mean, and do not spend other people's trust.

## Expected

- Critique the code, the rule, or the design. Leave the person out of it.
- Assume a spend record is private, even in a sample. Do not paste real names, card numbers, or keys into issues, pull requests, or chats.
- A review says what is wrong and what would fix it.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, slurs, or sexual comments.
- Publishing someone else's key, wallet seed, or unreleased spend export.
- Drive-by insults with no change attached.

## Reports

Email the maintainer or open a private security advisory for anything that involves a secret. For conduct, a private message is enough. Reports are read by the maintainer. A repeated or serious break can mean a blocked account and a closed pull request.

This is the project's own standard. It is not a substitute for the law.
